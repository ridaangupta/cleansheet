| 2026-10-09 | Vega Virtual Device / macOS | Capturing the VVD screen with `screencapture -l <window id>` (the Vega screenshot tool only supports hardware) | When the VVD window was minimized, behind another Space or otherwise off screen, the capture failed or returned a stale frame, and the scripted check had to be redone | 10 min | `scripts/vvd_screenshot.sh` only looks for on-screen windows and errors out clearly; keep the VVD window visible during scripted checks |
